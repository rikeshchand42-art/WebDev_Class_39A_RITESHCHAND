Introduction

Theodore Lindsey "Ted" "TJ" Templeton Jr., also known as The Boss Baby, BB, Double B, or Beebs, is the main character of the DreamWorks franchise The Boss Baby. He is known for his confident personality, leadership skills, and humorous nature.

Theodore plays an important role in several Boss Baby productions, including The Boss Baby, The Boss Baby: Back in Business, The Boss Baby: Family Business, and The Boss Baby: Back in the Crib.
